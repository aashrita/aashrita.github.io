Development of Antenna-Coupled Detectors and a Small Aperture Telescope for Cosmic Microwave Background Observations

Aashrita Mangu
Doctor of Philosophy in Physics
University of California, Berkeley, Spring 2024
Thesis Advisor: Adrian T. Lee

This dissertation was rather speedily written in 3 weeks after a 2 month deployment to Chile, where I was busy building the high-frequency small aperture telescope for the Simons Observatory. Does it sum up all the work I did? Most definitely not. Does it represent all the work I had the energy to type out? Yes, absolutely. Needless to say, I hope you enjoy reading it and catching all my errors ;) If I may recommend, my favorite chapter is Chapter 5 — easily the best chapter in this dissertation, and one I enjoyed the most writing in my limited time.
